## Coucou

ACT 3 adapts interaction and product patterns from [Coucou](https://github.com/louis-cfm/coucou) by Louis Raillé:

- Windows top-edge retract/peek behavior
- Compact, overview, and chat presentation states
- Tray-first/no-taskbar desktop companion behavior
- File-drop affordance and Mochi-style interaction cues

Coucou is MIT licensed. Copyright Louis Raillé 2026. The upstream project is available at the link above. This project includes the upstream Windows implementation as an adapted baseline under that MIT license; ACT 3 branding and the provider additions are the project-specific changes.
